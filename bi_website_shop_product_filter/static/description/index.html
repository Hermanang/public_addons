Website/WebShop Advanced Product Filter Odoo Apps

Odoo Webshop/Webstore Advanced Product Filter Odoo apps help your customer to apply advance/extended product filters option on webshop/ecommarce/online store in Odoo, where website visitor can easily and quickly search products by category and product with its attributes i.e color and brand to save their time . This odoo module also helps to search range of products ans once the search filter apply, it will automatic refresh the product list with auto reload page.
